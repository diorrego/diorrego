My scientific and statistical foundations, built through applied organizational research.

Research on workplace wellbeing

Universidad de Concepción · 2018 · Clínica Dental Cumbre Sur

I helped establish the second Happiness Management Department in Chile, at Clínica Dental Cumbre Sur, and served as Happiness Director for Concepción and Temuco. This research grew out of that work: turning organizational wellbeing into something we could measure, model and manage.

The management model connected healthy organizational practices, a strategic map and a Balanced Scorecard. For me, the research established a way of working with hypotheses, evidence and statistical rigor that I carry into product creation.

Original title: Análisis del bienestar de los colaboradores de una clínica dental.

Read the full research (PDF)

Structural equation modeling · SEM

Individual-level study
174 staff respondents across Concepción and Temuco.
Measurement instruments
PERMA, HERO and the Maslach Burnout Inventory.
Statistical methods
Structural equation modeling (SEM), maximum likelihood and Mann–Whitney comparisons.
Analysis tools
IBM SPSS Statistics and AMOS.

Global PERMA model → emotional exhaustion

The global PERMA model did not achieve acceptable fit: GFI 0.659, RMSEA 0.146. I then tested the components separately.

Component SEM models → emotional exhaustion

The component models reported inverse associations with emotional exhaustion. Positive emotions and meaning showed the largest associations; positive relationships showed a smaller association (p = 0.013).

Methods and interpretation

These are the standardized associations reported in the SEM results, not causal reductions in burnout. The study modeled emotional exhaustion, a dimension of burnout, with 174 respondents. The full initial model and the global PERMA–exhaustion model failed fit criteria. Component-model fit varied: positive emotions had RMSEA 0.087, above the study’s 0.08 threshold; the other four had RMSEA between 0.065 and 0.078.
