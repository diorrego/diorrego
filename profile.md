## Applied research and statistical foundations

In 2018, I developed research at Universidad de Concepción on wellbeing at Clínica Dental Cumbre Sur. I helped establish Chile's second Happiness Management Department and served as Happiness Director for Concepción and Temuco. This work established scientific and statistical foundations that I carry into product creation: hypotheses, measurement, evidence and interpretation.

The research connected healthy organizational practices, a strategic map and a Balanced Scorecard. It used PERMA, HERO and the Maslach Burnout Inventory, structural equation modeling (SEM), maximum likelihood, IBM SPSS Statistics and AMOS.

The individual-level study involved 174 staff respondents. The global PERMA model relating wellbeing to emotional exhaustion failed fit criteria: GFI 0.659 and RMSEA 0.146. Component models reported these standardized associations:

| PERMA component | Association with emotional exhaustion | GFI | RMSEA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positive emotions | -0.512 | 0.941 | 0.087 |
| Engagement | -0.481 | 0.950 | 0.070 |
| Positive relationships | -0.251 | 0.949 | 0.078 |
| Meaning | -0.508 | 0.954 | 0.065 |
| Accomplishment | -0.361 | 0.952 | 0.070 |

Positive emotions and meaning showed the largest inverse associations; positive relationships showed a smaller association (p = 0.013). These are reported associations, not causal reductions in burnout. Emotional exhaustion is a dimension of burnout, not the entire syndrome. Positive emotions had RMSEA 0.087, above the study's 0.08 threshold; the other four component models had RMSEA between 0.065 and 0.078. The full initial model also failed fit criteria.

Source: [Análisis del bienestar de los colaboradores de una clínica dental, 2018, public PDF](https://repositorio.udec.cl/server/api/core/bitstreams/44fc5fab-5b09-49b1-b5a6-4e13c93eaa0d/content).
